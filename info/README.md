# Information and meaning

Last reviewed: 2026-11-19

A family index. What a message carries, how it's written down, packed, protected and read, and what it can't tell you. It runs from what information is to what changes when someone on the channel is working against you.

## In reading order

In the order they build on one another. Each line says where the spine first needs the page.

1. [Information](information.md): what a message actually carries, why packing it tighter makes it fragile, and why it only means anything to someone who shares the codebook. First needed on [page 0](../00-representations.md).
2. [Encodings](encodings.md): how anything is represented as bytes, and why bytes mean nothing on their own. First needed on [page 0](../00-representations.md).
3. [Compression](compression.md): making data smaller by removing what's repeated, or what someone has decided you won't miss, and who that someone was thinking of. First needed on [page 11](../11-software-that-must-not-go-down.md).
4. [Error detection and correction](error-correction.md): adding redundancy in a structured way, so a damaged message can tell you it's damaged, and sometimes how to fix itself. First needed on [page 5](../05-machines-need-to-talk.md).
5. [Language as compression](language.md): why a sentence can carry so much, why it loses so much, and why writing requirements, prompts and handovers is mostly the work of putting back what words leave out. First needed on [page 12](../12-machines-that-learn-from-data.md).
6. [Meaning as use](meaning-as-use.md): an interface means whatever the people using it rely on, not what its documentation says. First needed on [page 3](../03-programs-too-big-for-one-head.md).
7. [Sampling and observation](observation.md): data only contains what something was built to notice, when it happened to look, and silence is not good news. First needed on [page 11](../11-software-that-must-not-go-down.md).
8. [Measures and targets](measures-and-targets.md): a measure is a sign that points at something you care about, and rewarding people or machines for moving the sign makes it drift from the thing. First needed on [page 11](../11-software-that-must-not-go-down.md).
9. [Information gain](information-gain.md): how to choose the next question, so each answer rules out as much as possible. First needed on [page 12](../12-machines-that-learn-from-data.md).
10. [Communication with adversaries](secrecy.md): what changes when someone on the channel is working against you. First needed on [page 7](../07-strangers-on-the-network.md).
