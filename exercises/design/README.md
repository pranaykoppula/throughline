# System design exercises

Design cases, two for each kind of system: read-heavy feeds, write-heavy metrics and logs, real-time messaging, money that must be exact, search, location and matching, large media, and coordination. Each one gives requirements and rough numbers, works through them step by step, shows two defensible designs and what each gives up, and ends by asking what breaks at ten times the size.

They'll be written after the system design pages they build on.
